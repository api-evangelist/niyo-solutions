Studying abroad is an exciting journey filled with new learning, diverse cultures, and endless opportunities. But even as students settle into life in a new country, one essential requirement never changes: the need for a smooth, secure, and affordable way to receive funds from home. From tuition payments and monthly living expenses to sudden emergencies, families often search for the most efficient way to send money internationally without long wait times or high transfer costs.
