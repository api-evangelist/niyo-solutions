The Kuwaiti Dinar (KWD) stands as the highest currency in the world . Most people think the US dollar or Euro holds this position, but facts tell us something different. As of 2026, the KWD is valued at approximately 3.25 USD, a position secured by the Central Bank of Kuwait’s policy to peg the dinar to an undisclosed weighted basket of international currencies rather than solely to the US dollar.
