Understanding Foreign Exchange Rate Determinants: A Beginner-friendly Breakdown The foreign exchange market, where currencies are bought, sold, and priced, is the largest financial market in the world . In fact, as of April 2025 , global FX trading reached a record average daily turnover of around US $9.6 trillion , up 28% from 2022 . This massive scale shows just how important exchange rates are to global trade, travel, and investment.
