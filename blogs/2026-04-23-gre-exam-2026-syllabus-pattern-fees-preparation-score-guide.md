What is the GRE Test? The GRE, or Graduate Record Examination, is a standardised test conducted by ETS (Educational Testing Service) and accepted by thousands of graduate and business school programs worldwide. It evaluates a candidate’s readiness for advanced academic studies by assessing verbal reasoning, quantitative reasoning, and analytical writing skills.
