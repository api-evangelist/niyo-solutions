What is a Foreign Exchange Rate? Demystifying Currency Values Summary A foreign exchange rate is the value of one country’s currency against another, determining how much you receive when converting money. The fluctuation of exchange rates is due to factors like inflation, interest rates, trade demand, geopolitics, and central bank decisions.
