Travelling abroad means you’ll likely spend in foreign currencies, whether that’s paying for a hotel, dining at a restaurant, shopping online from an overseas merchant, or withdrawing cash from a local ATM. One tool that’s been widely used by Indian travellers is the multi currency forex card travel card. In this blog, we’ll take a detailed look at how these cards work, their benefits and limitations, and why, in many cases, choosing a zero forex markup debit/credit card may be a more beneficial strategy.
