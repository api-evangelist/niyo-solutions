What is Foreign Currency? Types & Rates Explained In an increasingly connected world, understanding what is foreign currency and how it works is essential, whether you are travelling abroad, studying overseas, running an import–export business, or investing internationally. Foreign currency forms the backbone of global trade and finance, and its value changes constantly based on economic and market forces.
