Currency Exchange in India: How to Get the Best Rates Without Hidden Markup With international travel, overseas education, and global online shopping on the rise, currency exchange has become a routine financial need for many Indians. Yet, despite its frequency, foreign exchange remains one of the least understood areas of personal finance, and it can be surprisingly costly. A study found that Indian consumers paid over ₹263 billion in foreign exchange fees in 2020, of which about ₹97 billion were hidden as exchange rate markups on currency conversions, payments, and card purchases.
