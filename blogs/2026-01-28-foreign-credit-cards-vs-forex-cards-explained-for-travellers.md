Foreign Credit Cards vs Forex Cards Explained: Fees, Benefits & Best Use Cases Travelling abroad or making international purchases requires more than just planning your itinerary; it demands a smart payment strategy too. When it comes to handling money overseas, two popular choices for Indians are foreign credit cards and prepaid forex cards. Recent industry insights show that up to 80–90 % of corporate travellers now prefer using a forex card over other payment methods when spending abroad, highlighting the increasing shift towards prepaid solutions for cost management and transparency.
